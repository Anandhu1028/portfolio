Case Studies

Problems solved, results delivered

A closer look at the problem, the build, and the outcome for four representative projects.

CRM

TIMS CRM — Student Management System

Problem

The organization managed leads, student admissions, course enrollments, payments, accounting, invoices, offer letters, and follow-ups across spreadsheets, WhatsApp, and multiple disconnected tools. This resulted in duplicated data, delayed follow-ups, manual reporting, and limited visibility into business operations.

Solution

Developed a complete Laravel-based Education ERP and CRM that manages the entire student lifecycle—from lead generation to admission, course enrollment, payment collection, accounting, document management, invoice generation, offer letter automation, notifications, executive performance tracking, reporting, and role-based access control. The system also integrates call recordings, AI-powered call summaries, WhatsApp automation, and advanced analytics dashboards.

Laravel PHP MySQL Bootstrap 5 JavaScript AJAX Role & Permissions WhatsApp API

Business Result

Unified lead management, student administration, course management, finance, accounting, invoicing, admissions, and reporting into a single platform. Automated manual workflows, improved operational efficiency, enabled real-time reporting, and provided management with complete visibility into admissions, payments, executive performance, and business growth.

Student Management CRM

Student Management System

Problem

Student admissions, document verification, course enrollment, payment processing, and academic records were managed through multiple spreadsheets and manual workflows. Teams struggled to track admission progress, verify documents, manage balances, generate invoices, and coordinate between departments.

Solution

Developed a comprehensive Laravel-based Student Management System that digitizes the complete admission lifecycle. The platform supports student registration, course enrollment, dynamic fee calculation, payment management, balance tracking, document uploads and verification, role-based workflows for BDE, CRE, Accounts, and Administrators, payment approval and rejection, automatic Track ID generation, offer letter generation, invoice creation, notifications, and a secure student portal for accessing personal records and admission status.

Laravel PHP MySQL Bootstrap 5 JavaScript AJAX Role-Based Access PDF Generation

Business Result

Centralized the complete student admission and management process into a single platform. Automated admission workflows, document verification, fee calculation, payment approvals, invoice and offer letter generation, Track ID creation, and role-based collaboration, significantly improving operational efficiency, reducing manual work, and providing real-time visibility across admissions, accounts, and student services.

AI & Mobile Integration

AI Call Recording & Lead Intelligence

Problem

After leads were generated, sales executives contacted prospects using their mobile devices. Call recordings remained isolated from the CRM, making it difficult to associate conversations with the correct leads, review customer interactions, monitor call durations, or evaluate follow-up quality.

Solution

Developed a dedicated Android application integrated with a Laravel REST API to automatically upload call recordings to the server after each customer call. The backend validates recordings, removes duplicates, matches calls with the correct CRM lead using mobile numbers, records accurate call duration and timestamps, generates AI-powered call summaries, and displays the complete call history within the lead's timeline for sales teams and managers.

Laravel Android REST API MySQL AI Integration Speech-to-Text Call Recording Lead Matching

Business Result

Automated the entire call management workflow by synchronizing mobile call recordings with the CRM, eliminating duplicate records, accurately linking conversations to the corresponding leads, generating AI-powered summaries, and providing managers with searchable call history, performance insights, and complete visibility into customer communications.
